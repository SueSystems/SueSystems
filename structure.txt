SueSystems Website Development
Adviced to build
Studio Brand Website due to ease of Scalability, personal credibility and launching ventures. It requires balancing founder visibility with the brand identity.
How to Execute This on The Website
1. Master the Tone ("I" vs. "We")
•	On product & engineering pages: Identity is around the brand.
"SueSystems builds focused web applications and modular software solutions."
•	On the About page: Bring in the human element.
"Founded by Susan Omondi, SueSystems is a software development studio dedicated to engineering practical applications and contributing to open-source systems."
2. Website Structure
•	Homepage: Focus on what SueSystems builds, featured projects, and core development capabilities.
•	Projects / Products: Showcase applications, client work, and active builds under the brand umbrella.
•	About / Founder Section: Tell your story, your engineering philosophy, and the vision for growing SueSystems into a broader institution.
•	Contact: Use a clear professional address (e.g., contact@suesystems.com or hello@suesystems.com).

Section-by-Section Content Map
1. Hero Section (Above the Fold)
•	Heading: Engineering practical software solutions and scalable web systems.
•	Sub-heading: SueSystems is an independent development studio focused on building modular web applications, client solutions, and open-source software.
•	Call to Action (CTA) Buttons: View Projects | Get in Touch
2. Core Focus Areas (What SueSystems Does)
Keep this focused on outcomes and capabilities:
•	Custom Web Applications: Modern, responsive web apps designed with clean architecture and maintainability in mind.
•	Enterprise & Modular Tools: Developing specialized software modules (e.g., procurement and systems management).
•	Open Source & Ecosystem: Contributing to open-source software and community-driven tools.
3. Featured Projects Showcase
Highlight the key projects under the SueSystems umbrella:
•	Project Spotlight (e.g., TenderQApp): A modular tender response and management tool for enterprise platforms. (Include current stage, e.g., In Development / Wireframing)
•	Client Systems: Web solutions built for business operations, highlighting clean design and reliable functionality.
4. Founder & Vision Section (The "About" Preview)
This bridges the gap between solo founder and future institution:
Led by Engineering Purpose
SueSystems was founded by Susan Omondi, a Systems Engineer driven by structured design, open collaboration, and building high-impact software. What starts as a founder-led studio is built on systems meant to scale into a broader software institution.
